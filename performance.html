Reading the table

What each line means

Profit factor
Gross profit divided by gross loss, after costs. Above 1.0 made money, below 1.0 lost it.
First half / second half
The same figure for each half of the test period. Similar halves suggest a steadier result than one strong half.
Largest drawdown, closed trades
The biggest fall from a high in the running total, counting only finished trades.
Largest drawdown, open trades marked
The same, with open trades valued at their worst point along the way. Trailing loss limits work like this, so it's the one to compare with your account.
Worst day
The biggest one-day loss, with open trades marked the same way.

What every test has in common

  • Historical CME data for NQ and MNQ, in Eastern time, over the periods in the table.
  • Costs deducted on every trade, meant to cover commission and slippage. Your costs may be higher.
  • One contract on every trade, or a base of one where a strategy uses twice the base size on some trades. No compounding.
  • Code checks: each strategy's NinjaTrader file was run against its research model, trade for trade. Results in NinjaTrader's Strategy Analyzer, and in live trading, can differ because of data and fill differences.
