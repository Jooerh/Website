How we check

Read from the firm’s own site, every 6 hours

  1. We read each firm’s site. Its home page, promo banner and price pages, in a real browser when the page draws its prices with scripts. Never code sites or other people’s lists.
  2. A code is confirmed only when the firm shows it. The code and its discount have to be on the firm’s page, and the list prices have to match. Green means that happened within the last 12 hours.
  3. Anything unclear waits for a person. A new code that reads cleanly goes up at once. A change that doesn’t is held, and the card says when the code was last confirmed.

Where a firm shows its own price with the code, we show the firm’s number; otherwise we work it out from the list price and the discount, to the cent. Where a firm doesn’t state the discount, we show its list price and its code. Profit splits are each firm’s stated share of a payout to you, read on its own site on Nov 23, 2026; the check every 6 hours doesn’t reread them. A live account can pay a different split: each firm’s page here has the terms we read, with a link to them. Firms change offers without notice; the price at checkout is the one that counts.
